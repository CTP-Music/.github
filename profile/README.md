**Tesla predicted. Drazen discovered. Resofactor continues.**

It describes two energy units, C and T, and the processes that render physical reality, which is why consciousness, matter, time, gravity and propulsion all sit downstream of them.

CTP Music is the music and creative arm of CTP, carrying Conscious Translational Physics into the cultures already reaching for it: psytrance, EDM, New Age, psychedelic, holistic and consciousness-led music and art.

Music is not a side project to that. It is how the new science and the new consciousness from C-domain reach people who may never read a physics paper.

The portal lives at https://ctp-music.github.io/

## The portal

CTP Music Portal. A curated music and media portal for the global psytrance, EDM, New Age and psychedelic communities, alongside the wider consciousness, holistic and cutting-edge creative scene. Not a label and not a streaming service. A home for that audience's music, art and creative work, carrying the CTP name into a culture that already exists and already spends.

### Community submissions

The portal takes in work from the communities it serves rather than only publishing what comes from inside CTP. Creators put their material forward and it is curated rather than dumped: a portal with a standard is worth more to an artist than an open shelf nobody reads.

### Creative catalogue

The music, artwork and media of the CTP audience and its collaborators in one place, linkable without an account.

## How it is built

The site and catalogue are served from GitHub Pages. Anything that takes a submission, stores a file or processes an upload runs through a Vercel backend, because Pages serves static files only and cannot accept input.

The build runs as an open-source project, in the same spirit as CTP-EOS. The people who care about this culture are the same people who can contribute to it, and building in the open lets them do that rather than watch. Near-zero build cost, and contributors who arrive already invested in the outcome.

GitHub: https://github.com/CTP-Music

## Why music, and why first

Of everything in the CTP stack, music is the shortest distance to an audience that already exists, already gathers, and already spends. A physics claim invites evaluation. A track invites participation. The portal does the same job CTP-EOS does: it gives the audience something they actually want, and the CTP name travels with it.
