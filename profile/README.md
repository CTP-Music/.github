# CTP Music

Tesla predicted. Drazen discovered. Resofactor continues.

CTP Music is the music and creative arm of CTP, carrying Conscious Translational Physics into the cultures already reaching for it: psytrance, EDM, New Age, psychedelic, holistic and consciousness-led music and art.

CTP is the science the late Drazen Premate discovered and the body of work Resofactor carries forward as heir and successor to Nikola Tesla. It describes two energy units, C and T, and the processes that render physical reality, which is why consciousness, matter, time, gravity and propulsion all sit downstream of them.

Music is not a side project to that. It is how the new science new consciousness from C-domain reaches people who will (or might/may) never read a physics paper.

The portal lives at ctp-music.github.io/

## The portal

CTP Music Portal. A curated music and media portal for the global psytrance, EDM, New Age and psychedelic communities, alongside the wider consciousness, holistic and cutting-edge creative scene. Not a label and not a streaming service. A home for that audience's music, art and creative work, carrying the CTP name into a culture that already exists and already spends.

Community submissions. The portal takes in work from the communities it serves rather than only publishing what comes from inside CTP. Creators put their material forward and it is curated rather than dumped: a portal with a standard is worth more to an artist than an open shelf nobody reads.

Creative catalogue. The music, artwork and media of the CTP audience and its collaborators in one place, linkable without an account.

## How it is built

The site and catalogue are served from GitHub Pages. Anything that takes a submission, stores a file or processes an upload runs through a Vercel backend, because Pages serves static files only and cannot accept input.

The build runs as an open-source project, in the same spirit as CTP-EOS. The people who care about this culture are the same people who can contribute to it, and building in the open lets them do that rather than watch. Near-zero build cost, and contributors who arrive already invested in the outcome.

GitHub: github.com/CTP-Music

## Why music, and why first

Of everything in the CTP stack, music is the shortest distance to an audience that already exists, already gathers, and already spends. The psytrance and electronic music economy runs in the hundreds of millions to low billions annually on its own, and the wellness, consciousness and festival audiences around it are larger and better funded than most technology investors assume.

A physics claim invites evaluation. A track invites participation. The portal does the same job CTP-EOS does: it gives the audience something they actually want, and the CTP name travels with it.

## Inside the CTPVerse

CTP Music sits inside the CTPVerse with the rest of the ecosystem: CTP Energy and the science, CTP Space Systems and the craft, CTP Materials Sciences, CTP-EOS and the software group, CTP University, and the consumer, wellness and infrastructure units. It is not a separate company with a loose association. It is one science with several applications, each one a market the science opens rather than a business that happens to be nearby.

Capital first, research second, craft third governs the deep tech. The music portal is the near-term layer that builds audience and brand while the science runs on its longer horizon, and it is deliberately built to cost almost nothing to stand up.
